Spotify (Optional)

Voice-controlled music playback. "Play jazz", "Skip this song", "What's playing?" Full-track streaming requires a Spotify Premium account.

Step A — Register an app on Spotify (one-time)
1. Visit developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in.
2. Click Create App. Name: TapInsight, Description: AR glasses music control.
3. Under Redirect URIs, click the Copy button next to the URI in the dark box below, paste it into the Spotify field, then click Add. It must match exactly — same scheme (whichever the dark box shows, http:// or https://), same host (127.0.0.1, NOT localhost), same port, no trailing slash.
4. Check Web API and Web Playback SDK under "Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?"
5. Click Save on the Spotify page. Open Settings, copy the Client ID and Client Secret, and paste them below. Click Save configuration at the bottom of this page before continuing.
Redirect URI to paste on Spotify (copy this verbatim):
http://127.0.0.1:19110/spotify/callback
If you see "redirect_uri: Not matching configuration" from Spotify after clicking Connect, it means the URI pasted in your Spotify app dashboard doesn't exactly match this line. Copy it with the button above and make sure the URI in your Spotify app (and that you clicked Save on the dashboard) matches character-for-character.
Both values are in your app's Settings page on the Spotify Developer Dashboard. Tap Save configuration at the very bottom after entering them.
Step B — Connect your Spotify account
After saving your Client ID and Secret above, click Connect Spotify. A new tab will open and ask you to log in and authorize TapInsight. Once you see the success page you can close that tab and come back here.
Spotify connection status: loading…
Tip: say "Play Fleetwood Mac" to TapInsight. A Spotify player opens on the glasses with fast-forward and rewind controls for the queue.
Important — keep the Spotify app running on your phone.
The glasses' WebView often can't stream full Spotify tracks on its own (Spotify's Web Playback SDK requires Widevine/EME that the X3 WebView doesn't always expose). When that happens, TapInsight automatically hands playback off to your phone via Spotify Connect, and you hear the music on the glasses through the existing Bluetooth pairing.

For this hand-off to work, the Spotify app must be open and running on the phone you want to play on (background or foreground is fine). If your phone isn't currently listed as a Connect device, open Spotify on it and start any track for a second — that registers it as active — then press Refresh in the player on the glasses. The same applies if you'd rather route to a desktop or speaker: that target's Spotify instance needs to be running first.

Song Identification — AudD (Optional)

Ask "what song is this?" while listening to TapRadio. With an AudD token, TapInsight asks AudD to fingerprint the current stream first so stale station metadata does not win. Without a token, TapInsight falls back to the free track metadata broadcast by many stations. Get a free token (300 IDs/month) at audd.io.

Leave blank to use only the free station-metadata path. Tap Save configuration at the bottom after entering it.

When set to Never interrupt, Gemini will always finish speaking before listening. Background noise and ambient sound will be completely ignored. Useful for noisy environments like museums, concerts, or crowded streets.
Controls how easily your voice interrupts Gemini while it is speaking. This affects both the on-device barge-in logic and the Gemini server-side voice detection.
Recommended levels:
• 0.6 – 1.0 — Quiet room, home office, library
• 1.0 – 1.5 — Normal indoor environment (default: 1.0)
• 1.5 – 2.0 — Busy office, café, light street noise
• 2.0 – 2.5 — Museum, airport, crowded venue, outdoor wind
Microphone audio below this level is treated as silence. Higher values filter out more background noise, preventing Gemini from being interrupted by ambient sounds. Measured in PCM16 peak amplitude.
Recommended levels:
• 100 – 300 — Very quiet room, recording studio
• 300 – 600 — Normal indoor environment
• 600 – 1200 — Busy indoor spaces, light street noise (default: 600)
• 1200 – 2000 — Museum, crowded venue, moderate outdoor noise
• 2000 – 3000 — Very noisy — only loud, close speech will register
