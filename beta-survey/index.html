TapInsight Feature Survey

Help us shape the future of AI-powered AR glasses for RayNeo X3 Pro

Welcome to the TapInsight v1.2 Public Beta feature survey. We've researched what AR glasses users want most and compiled the top feature candidates below.

Select the features you'd find most valuable, rank your top 3, and share any additional ideas. Your feedback directly shapes our development roadmap.

VERY HIGH DEMAND
🌐 Real-Time Translation
Live translation of speech and text seen through the camera. Support for 40+ languages with on-screen subtitle overlay. Say "translate" to activate, or enable always-on mode for travel.
HIGH DEMAND
💪 Fitness & Health Dashboard
Google Fit integration showing steps, heart rate, calories, and workout stats on the HUD. Voice commands like "how many steps today" or "start a workout". Posture reminders and hydration alerts.
VERY HIGH DEMAND
🔔 Smart Notification Filtering
Intelligent notification triage — choose which apps show on the HUD. Priority modes: "Focus" (only urgent), "Social" (messages + calls), "All". AI summarization of long notifications. Quick reply via voice.
VERY HIGH DEMAND
🔋 Battery Saver Mode
Extend battery life by 40-60%. Reduces HUD refresh rate, pauses background polling, dims display, and switches to lightweight voice-only mode. Auto-activates below configurable threshold (e.g. 20%).
HIGH DEMAND
⚡ Quick Actions & Voice Macros
Custom voice-triggered shortcuts. Define phrases like "good morning" to trigger daily briefing + weather + calendar. "Leaving work" to get traffic + ETA home. "Meeting mode" to enable DND + auto-notes.
HIGH DEMAND
🧠 Persistent AI Memory
AI that remembers your preferences, past conversations, and context across sessions. "Remember I'm allergic to peanuts" persists permanently. Builds a personal knowledge graph over time.
HIGH DEMAND
🔮 Proactive AI Suggestions
Context-aware nudges without being asked. Approaching a restaurant? Get ratings. Running late for a meeting? Traffic alert. At a store? Price comparison. Weather changing? Heads up before you leave.
MEDIUM DEMAND
📱 Phone Screen Mirroring
Mirror your phone's screen to the AR display. Read texts, check maps, or follow recipes hands-free. Supports gesture and voice control for basic navigation.
MEDIUM DEMAND
👥 Social & Contacts HUD
See caller ID, recent messages, and social media mentions on the HUD. AI-powered "who's that?" face recognition (opt-in) for events. Contact card overlay during calls.
MEDIUM DEMAND
📄 Document & QR Scanner Pro
Enhanced document scanning with OCR, auto-crop, and save-to-cloud. QR codes auto-scan and execute (open URLs, add contacts, join Wi-Fi). Business card scanner with contact import.
